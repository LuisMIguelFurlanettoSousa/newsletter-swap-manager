How it works — three steps

  1. 1. Match by audience, not by guess

    We index public newsletters by niche, size band, and recent growth signal. Tell us where you are; we surface compatible partners — and the platform they publish on.

  2. 2. Outreach with proven templates

    Copy a tested outreach template (Indiegraf-style or beehiiv-style), customize one paragraph, send. We track which template language drives reply rate — you copy what works.

  3. 3. Track UTM cross-platform

    One swap, one campaign, one attribution view — even when one of you is on Substack and the other is on beehiiv. Cliques, conv rate, subs added, retention 30/60/90d.
